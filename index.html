A typesetting game

ProofSheet

You get a printed page. Reproduce it in LaTeX before the clock runs out.

What counts
Your source is compiled by a real TeX engine and compared to the target pixel for pixel. Anything that typesets identically is accepted, so \textbf{x} and {\bfseries x} are equally right.
What you write
The document body only. The class and preamble are already set, with amsmath, tikz, xcolor and friends loaded.
Scoring
One point per ten characters of the target's source. Three minutes on the clock. Skipping costs nothing but time.

Loading a TeX Live distribution into your browser. This happens once.
